How it works

From discovery call to day one

No lengthy hiring process, no guesswork on fit. Here's exactly what happens between reaching out and having a VA handling real work.

01

Tell us what you need

We start with a short intake — your business, the tasks eating your time, and the hours you're looking to cover. No sales pitch, just enough for us to know what a good match looks like for you.

Day 0
02

We learn your business

We dig into your workflows, the tools you already use, and the kind of person who'd actually fit how your team operates — not just a skills checklist.

Day 1–2
03

We find your match

We shortlist from our vetted bench and introduce the VA whose skills and availability line up with your needs — typically within 24 hours of your intake call.

Within 24 hrs
04

You meet your VA

A short introduction call before anything starts, so you know exactly who you're working with and can flag anything that matters before day one.

Before day one
05

You delegate, we support

Your VA becomes part of the workflow — async updates, a standing check-in, and the option to adjust hours or request a rematch anytime, for as long as you're with us.

Ongoing

Before you book the call

Onboarding, answered

Can I choose my VA?

We match based on your specific needs, required skills, and working style rather than assigning at random — and you'll meet your VA before getting started.

What happens if my VA isn't the right fit?

We'll work with you to understand the issue and find the right next step — that's part of the service, not an extra ask.

How quickly can I get started?

Timing depends on the role and candidate availability. Once we understand your needs, we'll give you a realistic timeline.
