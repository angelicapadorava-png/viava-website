Transaction Coordinator

A Transaction Coordinator who keeps every deal organised from accepted offer to closing, so nothing is missed between contract and keys.

Transaction coordination is a specialist real estate position, not part of general executive support. It needs its own experience, tools and attention to deadlines. We match Transaction Coordinators separately and price them to the scope of your transactions.

Responsibilities

What your Transaction Coordinator handles.

You set the exact list during onboarding. These are the responsibilities this position is built around.

01

Contract to close

  • Opening the transaction file once an offer is accepted
  • Tracking key dates and contingency deadlines
  • Closing checklists kept current
02

Documents

  • Collecting, organising and checking that documents are complete
  • Requesting missing signatures and paperwork
  • Keeping the transaction platform up to date
03

Coordination

  • Timely updates to clients, agents, lenders, title or escrow and other parties, as you direct
  • Scheduling inspections, appraisals and walk-throughs
  • Status summaries for you

Typical tools: Dotloop · SkySlope · DocuSign · Your CRM · Google Workspace

Who it’s for

Is a Transaction Coordinator right for you?

What we look for in your Transaction Coordinator

  • Prior transaction coordination experience
  • Familiarity with transaction platforms such as Dotloop, SkySlope or DocuSign
  • Understanding of contract-to-close timelines in your market
  • Exceptional attention to detail and deadlines

Experience is matched to the position: not every VIA professional is placed in every role.

Not included in this position

  • Anything that requires a real estate licence, such as negotiating, giving advice, or interpreting contract terms for clients
  • Legal advice or drafting contract language
  • Signing documents on anyone’s behalf
  • Tasks your brokerage or state rules reserve for licensed agents

Questions

About the Transaction Coordinator role.

Why isn’t this part of Executive Assistant support?

Transaction coordination carries real deadlines and real risk. It needs someone who has done it before and knows the platforms, so we match it as its own specialist position.

How is it priced?

On a custom quote, based on the experience needed and your transaction volume and scope. The standard packages don’t apply automatically.

Does the coordinator replace my licensed responsibilities?

No. Your Transaction Coordinator works within your brokerage’s policies and your state’s rules. Anything requiring a licence stays with you.
