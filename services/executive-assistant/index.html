An example

What a typical day could look like

Every client’s routine is different. This is a typical pattern, so you can picture the hand-off.

  1. Inbox sorted and urgent items flagged before you start
  2. Meetings confirmed and calendar conflicts resolved
  3. A client document drafted and formatted for your review
  4. CRM records updated after the day’s calls
  5. End-of-day summary of what’s done and what needs you

Who it’s for

Is an Executive Assistant right for you?

What we look for in your Executive Assistant

  • Strong written and spoken English
  • Experience supporting an owner, executive or busy team
  • Comfortable with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, calendars and a CRM
  • Discretion with confidential information
  • Organised, proactive and clear in updates

Experience is matched to the position: not every VIA professional is placed in every role.

Not included in this position

  • Specialist roles such as transaction coordination or bookkeeping (separate positions)
  • Anything that requires a professional licence, such as legal, financial or real estate advice
  • Signing or approving on your behalf without your explicit instruction

Questions

About the Executive Assistant role.

Is administrative support included?

Yes. Executive Assistant support already covers administrative work: inbox, calendar, scheduling, documents, data upkeep and research. You don’t need a separate administrative assistant.

How is it priced?

Executive Assistants are available on our Essential (20 hours a week), Growth (30) and Dedicated (40) packages. See the pricing page.

Can my Executive Assistant handle transaction coordination?

Transaction coordination is a specialist real estate position with its own experience and tools. We match a Transaction Coordinator separately, on custom pricing.

Who directs the work?

You do. You set the priorities day to day, and agree how updates and check-ins work during onboarding.
