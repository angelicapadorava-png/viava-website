Specialist position

Bookkeeper

A Bookkeeper who keeps your records current, categorised and ready for your accountant.

Bookkeeping is a specialist position that needs proven experience with accounting software and careful handling of financial information. It supports, and doesn’t replace, your accountant or tax adviser.

Responsibilities

What your Bookkeeper handles.

You set the exact list during onboarding. These are the responsibilities this position is built around.

01

Day-to-day records

  • Recording and categorising transactions
  • Receipt and document organisation
  • Bank and card reconciliations
02

Billing support

  • Preparing and sending invoices you approve
  • Accounts receivable follow-ups
  • Bills prepared for your payment approval
03

Reporting

  • Monthly reports for your review
  • Keeping records ready for your accountant

Typical tools: QuickBooks Online · Xero · Your banking portal (view access as agreed) · Google Workspace

Who it’s for

Is a Bookkeeper right for you?

What we look for in your Bookkeeper

  • Proven bookkeeping experience
  • Proficiency in QuickBooks Online, Xero or the software you use
  • Accuracy and discretion with financial information

Experience is matched to the position: not every VIA professional is placed in every role.

Not included in this position

  • Tax preparation, tax filing or tax advice
  • Accounting, audit or financial advice that requires a licensed professional
  • Making payments or moving money without your approval
  • Payroll compliance decisions

Human-led, AI-enhanced

AI is part of how they work, not an add-on.

Where it helps, and only in tools you approve, your Bookkeeper may use AI to draft, summarise and organise. They check the work and own the result, and anything that needs your decision comes to you. The tools used, AI included, are the ones you provide or approve; specialist AI services and software subscriptions aren’t part of the packages.

Questions

About the Bookkeeper role.

Does a Bookkeeper replace my accountant?

No. Your Bookkeeper keeps the records; your accountant or tax adviser handles accounting advice, tax and filings.

How is it priced?

On a custom quote, based on the experience needed and your volume and scope.
