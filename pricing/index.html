Pricing questions

Something else? Ask us directly.

Which positions do the packages cover?

Our standard positions: Executive Assistant, Marketing Assistant, Customer Support Assistant. Specialist positions, such as Transaction Coordinator, Bookkeeper, Operations Specialist, are priced on a custom quote.

Is administrative support included with an Executive Assistant?

Yes. Executive Assistant support already covers administrative work, so there’s no separate administrative assistant to add.

Can I change my hours later?

Talk to us as your workload changes. We’ll review your hours together and confirm any change in writing.

How does billing work?

Each package is a flat monthly price. We go through billing and terms on your discovery call, and they’re set out in your written agreement before work starts.

Why are specialist positions priced separately?

Roles like transaction coordination and bookkeeping need specific experience, tools and responsibility. We quote them once we understand the experience required and your scope.

Who directs the work?

You do. You set the day-to-day priorities, and agree how updates and check-ins work during onboarding.

Not sure which package?

Tell us about your week. We’ll recommend a position and the hours that fit, with no obligation.
