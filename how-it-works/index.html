How it works

Calm, clear, and built around you.

Four stages, no surprises. You always know what happens next, what we need from you, and what you get.

  1. 01 Discovery
  2. 02 Matching
  3. 03 Onboarding
  4. 04 Working together
01

Discovery

A relaxed conversation about how your week actually runs: what fills it, what you’d hand off first, and the tools you already use.

You
Bring your messiest to-do list. No preparation needed.
We
Listen, ask good questions, and say honestly if we’re not the right fit.
You get
A clear picture of what could come off your plate.

What a first call often uncovers

  • Inbox takes six or more hours a week
  • Leads wait days for a reply
  • Uses Google Workspace and a CRM
  • Wants mornings free for clients
02

Matching

We recommend support that fits the work (the skills, the hours and the working style) and introduce you to the person we recommend.

You
Review the recommendation, ask anything, and decide.
We
Match on skills, tools, time zone and working style.
You get
Someone who fits how you work, not just a CV.

03

Onboarding

Access, tools and priorities are agreed up front, and the way you like things done gets written down, so nothing depends on memory.

You
Share access to the tools the work needs, and only those.
We
Document your preferences, approvals and escalation rules.
You get
A first week that already feels organised.

04

Working together

You direct the day-to-day work. Updates and check-ins follow the rhythm you agree during onboarding, and you can adjust as your business changes.

You
Set priorities, approve what matters and give quick feedback.
Your professional
Keeps the routine running and sends clear written updates.
You get
Written updates now, and a client portal we’re building.

Common questions

Something else? Ask us directly.

How long does matching take?

After your discovery call we share a recommendation and a realistic timeline. It depends on the skills and hours your role needs.

Who decides on the match?

You do. We recommend a professional for your work and introduce you; you decide whether to go ahead.

What tools will they use?

Yours. Your professional works inside the tools you already use, with access you control. AI tools are used only where you approve them.

What if the fit isn’t right?

Tell us. We’ll talk it through and work out the right next step with you.

What are the terms?

We explain the terms on the discovery call, and they’re set out in your written agreement before any work starts.

How do I know what’s been done?

Your professional sends regular written updates, and you agree a check-in rhythm during onboarding.
