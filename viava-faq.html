FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about hiring, pricing, VA quality, and how it all works day to day — before you ever talk to us.

Hiring

How does the matching process work?

You tell us what you need — the tasks, the hours, the kind of support — and we match you with a vetted VA whose skills and availability fit. You'll meet them before anything starts, so there are no surprises on day one.

How quickly can I get a VA?

Most clients are matched with a VA within 24 hours of their intake call.

Do I interview my VA?

You'll have an introduction call with your matched VA before you start working together, so you know exactly who you're working with and can flag anything that matters before day one.

Can I request a specific skill set?

Yes. Tell us the skills, software, or industry experience you need, and we match accordingly rather than assigning at random.

Are VAs dedicated to one client?

Yes. Your VA works with you 1:1 — dedicated, not pooled or shared across multiple clients.

Pricing

How much does a VA cost?

Part-Time support starts at $699/mo, Full-Time at $1,259/mo, and Specialized support (industry-specific expertise, multi-VA teams) is custom-quoted based on your needs.

Is there a setup fee?

Pricing is a flat monthly rate for your VA's time. We'll walk you through the specifics on your intro call so there are no surprises.

Are there long-term contracts?

No — VIA VA is month-to-month, with no long-term lock-in.

How do payments work?

Billing is monthly, tied to your plan. We'll cover invoicing and timing specifics during your intro call.

Can I increase/decrease hours?

Yes. Your plan can flex as your workload changes — just let your VA or our team know.

VA Quality

How do you vet VAs?

Every VA goes through screening and interviews before joining our bench, so you're only ever matched with people who are ready to work.

What experience do they have?

VAs are matched to your needs based on relevant skills and experience — from general admin to specialized work like real estate transaction coordination.

Where are your VAs located?

Our VAs are based in the Philippines, known for strong English-language skills and deep experience supporting U.S. and international businesses remotely.

What happens if my VA doesn't work out?

We'll work with you to understand the issue and find the right next step — that's part of the service, not an extra ask.

Is there a replacement/rematch guarantee?

Yes — every placement comes with a free rematch guarantee if the fit isn't right.

Working Together

What tools can my VA use?

Whatever you already use — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, your CRM, project management tools, and more. Your VA is onboarded on your specific setup.

What hours can they work?

Hours are built around what you need — we'll align on a schedule that works for your time zone and workflow during onboarding.

Who manages the VA?

You direct the day-to-day work; VIA VA stays involved behind the scenes to support performance and step in if anything needs attention.

How does communication work?

Directly with your VA, however works best for you — email, Slack, or your preferred tool — with our team available for anything that needs to be escalated.

What happens if I need additional support?

Just let us know. You can adjust hours, add specialized support, or bring on an additional VA as your needs grow.

Still have questions?

Let's talk it through.

Answer a few quick questions and a member of the VIA VA team will follow up within 24 hours.
