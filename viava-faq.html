FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about hiring, pricing, VA quality, and how it all works day to day—before you ever talk to us.

Get Matched With a VA

Hiring

How does the matching process work?

You tell us what you need—the tasks, the hours, the kind of support—and we match you with a vetted VA whose skills and availability fit. You'll meet them before anything starts.

How quickly can I get a VA?

Most clients are matched with a VA within 24 hours of their intake call.

Do I interview my VA?

You'll have an introduction call with your matched VA before you start working together.

Can I request a specific skill set?

Yes. Tell us the skills, software, or industry experience you need, and we match accordingly.

Are VAs dedicated to one client?

Yes. Your VA works with you 1:1—dedicated, not pooled or shared.

VA Quality

How do you vet VAs?

Every VA goes through screening and interviews before joining our bench, so you're only matched with people who are ready to work.

What experience do they have?

VAs are matched to your needs based on relevant skills and experience—from general admin to specialized work.

Are your VAs trained in AI?

Yes. Every VA is trained to use AI tools intentionally to save time and deliver better results.

What happens if my VA doesn't work out?

We'll work with you to understand the issue and find the right next step—that's part of the service.

Is there a replacement/rematch guarantee?

Yes—every placement comes with a free rematch guarantee if the fit isn't right.

Pricing

How much does a VA cost?

Part-Time support starts at $699/mo, Full-Time at $1,259/mo, and Specialized support is custom-quoted based on your needs.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Pricing is a flat monthly rate for your VA's time.

Are there long-term contracts?

No—VIA VA is month-to-month, with no long-term lock-in.

How do payments work?

Billing is monthly, tied to your plan. We'll cover invoicing specifics during your intro call.

Can I increase or decrease hours?

Yes. Your plan can flex as your workload changes—just let us know.

Working Together

What tools can my VA use?

Whatever you already use—Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, your CRM, project tools, and more. Your VA is onboarded on your setup.

What hours can they work?

Hours are built around what you need—we align on a schedule during onboarding.

Who manages the VA?

You direct the day-to-day work; VIA VA stays involved behind the scenes to support performance.

How does communication work?

Directly with your VA—email, Slack, or your preferred tool—with our team available for anything that needs escalating.

What if I need additional support?

Just let us know. You can adjust hours, add specialized support, or bring on an additional VA as your needs grow.
