Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we collect

  • When you send an inquiry: your name, email address, business name, website, industry, the support you’re interested in, the hours and start date you have in mind, and anything you choose to tell us.
  • When you request a free download: your email address and which download you asked for.
  • When you book a call: booking happens on Google Calendar’s appointment booking page, under Google’s privacy terms. We receive the details you enter there, such as your name and email address. Calls take place on Google Meet.
  • When you email us: whatever you include in your message.
  • Technical information: to protect our forms from spam and abuse, we record a one-way hashed version of your IP address, your browser type and the page you came from with each form submission.

This website doesn’t use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and talk with you about our services.
  • To send you the download you asked for, and occasional VIA resources if you agreed to that when you requested it.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with clients and prospective clients.
  • To keep our forms secure and free of spam.

We ask for your consent on each form before anything is sent.

Where it’s kept and who sees it

Inquiries and download requests are stored in VIA’s private client-management system, which only authorised VIA staff can access, and are sent to our team by email. We use service providers to run this: our website host, our email provider, our database and hosting provider, and Google (Calendar and Meet) for bookings and calls. We don’t sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or tell us to stop sending you resources, at any time. Email hello@viavateam.com and we’ll respond.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your information: hello@viavateam.com.
