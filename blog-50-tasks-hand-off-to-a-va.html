Delegation & VA Management

50 Tasks You Can Hand Off to a VA This Week

A practical list of tasks you can delegate today to free your time, reduce overwhelm, and focus on growing your business.

By VIA VA Team  ·  8 min read

Your time is better spent on work only you can do.

Delegation isn't about offloading busywork—it's about creating the space you need to focus on strategy, growth, and the parts of your business that only you can lead.

Here are 50 practical tasks you can hand off to a virtual assistant this week. Pick a few, delegate with confidence, and get your time back.

If a task is repeatable, teachable, and doesn't require your personal authority, it may be ready to delegate.

01 Administrative Tasks

  1. 1. Inbox organization and email filtering
  2. 2. Calendar management and appointment scheduling
  3. 3. Data entry and spreadsheet updates
  4. 4. File organization and cloud folder management
  5. 5. Meeting scheduling and agenda preparation
  6. 6. Document formatting and proofreading

02 Marketing & Social Media

  1. 1. Social media scheduling and posting
  2. 2. Canva graphics and visual content creation
  3. 3. Caption writing and post copy
  4. 4. Content repurposing (blog → social media, etc.)
  5. 5. Email newsletter creation and sending
  6. 6. Analytics reports and performance tracking

03 Client Support

  1. 1. Responding to inquiries and FAQs
  2. 2. CRM updates and contact management
  3. 3. Follow-up reminders and thank you emails
  4. 4. Onboarding support for new clients
  5. 5. Client reports and monthly updates

04 Systems & Operations

  1. 1. SOP documentation and process creation
  2. 2. Project tracking and task management
  3. 3. Research and vendor comparisons
  4. 4. Database cleanup and duplicate removal
  5. 5. Weekly reporting and KPI updates

05 Getting Started

You don't need to delegate everything at once. The businesses that delegate well start small and build from there.

Start small

Choose 3–5 tasks from this list, document how you do them, and delegate with clear instructions. Small steps create big time savings.
