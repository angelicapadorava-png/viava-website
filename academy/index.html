VIA Academy

Where VIA professionals keep growing.

We’re building VIA Academy as a learning platform and a professional community in one: video lessons, structured upskilling paths, weekly practice challenges, a private community and practical resources for doing excellent client work.

01 · Learn

Short lessons built around real tasks.

A video library and courses that teach the work VIA clients actually hand over, organised into upskilling paths so every professional knows what to learn next.

Paths we’re designing

Real estate supportLeads, CRM, showings, listings and transaction admin, done the way agents need.
Client communicationWriting clearly, following up on time, and representing a client’s voice well.
AI-assisted workflowsUsing AI inside real tasks, and checking what it produces.
Systems and automationCRM set-up, connected tools and documenting how things run.

02 · Practise

Weekly challenges that build judgment.

Knowing isn’t the same as doing. Each week brings a short, realistic task. Professionals submit their work and get feedback, so skills grow through practice rather than quizzes.

  1. The briefA realistic task, like turning a messy listing brief into a clear checklist.
  2. The submissionDone the way you’d do it for a client.
  3. The feedbackSpecific notes on what worked and what to sharpen.

03 · Belong

A community that has your back.

Remote work can be lonely. The Academy community gives VIA professionals private spaces to ask questions, share what works and celebrate progress with people who do the same work.

  • Private to VIA professionalsDiscussions aren’t public and aren’t visible to clients.
  • Client confidentiality comes firstNo client names, data or documents in community spaces.
  • ModeratedClear guidelines, reporting, and moderators to keep it helpful and kind.

04 · Grow

Progress you can see.

Tips, templates and practical guides alongside each path, plus a clear view of progress and achievements, so growth is visible to the professional, and to clients when the professional chooses to share it.

  • ResourcesTemplates, checklists and tips that sit next to the lessons they support.
  • Progress and achievementsPaths completed and skills practised, recorded as you go.
  • Shared with consentVerified, job-relevant milestones can be shared with a client, only if the professional agrees.

Part of VIA HQ

One connected place, not another app.

VIA Academy is being built inside VIA HQ, the secure system VIA already uses to run the business. When it opens, VIA professionals will sign in there with the account VIA sets up for them.

  • By invitationAccess is for VIA professionals only. There’s nothing to sign up for here.
  • One sign-inNo separate Academy account or password.
  • Private by designClients can’t see Academy discussions, training records or assessments.

Questions about VIA Academy

Who is VIA Academy for?

VIA professionals: the people who support VIA clients. Access is by invitation.

Is it open yet?

Not yet. We’re building it in stages and will invite VIA professionals as each part is ready.

How will professionals sign in?

Through VIA HQ, with the account VIA sets up for them. There’s no separate Academy account to create.

Can clients see Academy discussions or training records?

No. Discussions, submissions, feedback and training records stay private to the Academy. A professional can choose to share verified milestones with their client.

What does VIA Academy mean for my business?

The professional supporting you keeps building practical skills, from client communication to AI-assisted workflows. If they choose, they can share verified milestones with you.
