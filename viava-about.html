About VIA VA

We're building a better way to work

Modern, flexible, people-first remote support — for businesses that need it, and for the virtual professionals who provide it.

VIA VA started with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to choose between doing everything themselves or building a large internal team. There's a third option — modern, flexible, people-first remote support.

We connect businesses with virtual professionals who take work off their plate, while creating real career opportunities for talented people working remotely.

Our mission

FAQ

Before you get started

What is a virtual assistant?

A remote professional providing administrative, operational, creative, customer service, sales, or specialized support.

Why hire through VIA VA instead of independently?

Hiring independently means you handle sourcing, screening, interviewing, and onboarding yourself. VIA VA does that structured matching for you.

Are VIA VA assistants based in the Philippines?

Yes — our VAs are based in the Philippines, known for strong English-language skills and deep experience supporting U.S. and international businesses remotely.

Can I choose my VA?

We match based on your specific needs, required skills, and working style rather than assigning at random — and you'll meet your VA before getting started.

Part-time or full-time?

Both. Support is structured around your workload and requirements.

What happens if my VA isn't the right fit?

We'll work with you to understand the issue and find the right next step — that's part of the service, not an extra ask.

How quickly can I get started?

Timing depends on the role and candidate availability. Once we understand your needs, we'll give you a realistic timeline.
